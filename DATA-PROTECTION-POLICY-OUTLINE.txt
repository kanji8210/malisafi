1) PURPOSE

- Explain why this policy exists.

==================================================
2) SCOPE
==================================================
- Applies to: employees, contractors, vendors, partners, and systems.
- Applies to: websites, apps, plugins, databases, cloud services, and paper records.
- Applies to personal data processed during business operations.

==================================================
3) DEFINITIONS
==================================================
- Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
- Sensitive Data: Data requiring higher protection (e.g., ID info, precise location, financial data).
- Processing: Collection, use, storage, sharing, deletion, etc.
- Data Subject: Individual whose data is being processed.
- Data Controller / Processor: Roles and responsibilities in processing.

==================================================
4) DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES
==================================================
- Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency.
- Purpose limitation (collect for specific, legitimate purposes only).
- Data minimization (only what is necessary).
- Accuracy (keep data updated and correct).
- Storage limitation (retain only as long as needed).
- Integrity and confidentiality (security controls in place).
- Accountability (document and prove compliance).

==================================================
5) LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
==================================================
- Consent.
- Contract performance.
- Legal obligation.
- Legitimate interests.
- Public task / vital interests (if applicable).
- Note: Keep a processing activity register with legal basis per activity.

==================================================
6) PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT
==================================================
- Identity data: name, username, account ID.
- Contact data: email, phone, address.
- Property/listing data: listing details, county/subcounty/neighbourhood, media uploads.
- Technical data: IP address, device/browser details, logs, cookies.
- Transactional data: subscription/payment references (avoid storing raw card data).
- Support data: inquiries, reports, customer support communications.

==================================================
7) HOW WE COLLECT DATA
==================================================
- Directly from users via forms, account registration, and submissions.
- Automatically via logs, cookies, analytics, and security tools.
- From third parties (payment gateways, identity verification, integrations).
