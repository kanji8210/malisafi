## Introduction

The Malisafi MLS Fraud Reporting System helps maintain a safe and trustworthy platform for all users. If you encounter suspicious activity, fake listings, or misleading information, you can report it through our easy-to-use reporting form.

## How to Report Fraud

### Step 1: Access the Report Form

Navigate to the Fraud Report page (usually at `/report-fraud/`) or click "Report Fraud" links available throughout the platform.

### Step 2: Select the Type of Fraud

Choose the category that best describes the issue from the dropdown:

- **Fake Listing** - Property doesn't exist or is not for sale
- **Duplicate Property** - Same property listed multiple times
- **Misleading Information** - Incorrect details about property
- **Fake Agent** - Agent profile is fraudulent or impersonated
- **Price Scam** - Unrealistic or deceptive pricing
- **Fake Photos** - Images don't match property or are stolen
- **Contact Fraud** - Phone/email information is fake
- **Identity Theft** - Someone using another person's identity
- **Spam** - Repeated or irrelevant postings
- **Other** - Any other fraudulent activity

### Step 3: Identify the Agent or Property

You can optionally specify:

- **Agent Involved**: Start typing the agent's name to search. The system will suggest matching agents.
- **Property Involved**: Start typing the property name or address. Matching properties will appear.

**Note**: You must select at least one (agent OR property).

### Step 4: Provide Your Email (if not logged in)

If you're not logged in, you'll need to provide your email address. This is used only to:
- Contact you if we need clarification
- Notify you about the investigation status

**Your email is kept confidential** and never shared with the reported party.

### Step 5: Describe the Issue

- **Brief Description** (required): Summarize the problem in one sentence (max 500 characters)
- **Detailed Information** (required): Provide as much detail as possible:
  - What did you observe?
  - When did this occur?
  - Any communications or evidence you have
  - Screenshots descriptions (if applicable)

**Tip**: The more specific you are, the better we can investigate.

### Step 6: Submit Your Report

Click **Submit Report**. You'll receive a confirmation message if successful.

## Report Types Explained

### Fake Listing
Property doesn't exist, is not actually for sale, or details are completely fabricated.

**Example**: An apartment advertised as available but doesn't exist at the listed address.

### Duplicate Property
Same property posted multiple times by same or different agents.

**Example**: Villa in Westlands listed three times with different prices.

### Misleading Information
Property details are inaccurate or exaggerated.

**Example**: Property listed as "oceanfront" but is actually 5km from the beach.

### Fake Agent
Agent profile uses fake credentials, stolen identity, or impersonation.

**Example**: Agent using photos and name of a real person without permission.

### Price Scam
Price significantly below or above market value to deceive buyers/sellers.

**Example**: Luxury apartment listed at KES 10,000/month (unrealistically low).

### Fake Photos
Images stolen from other sources or don't match the actual property.

**Example**: Photos from a hotel website used for a residential property.

### Contact Fraud
Phone numbers or email addresses are fake or lead to scammers.

**Example**: Agent's phone goes to unrelated business or is disconnected.

### Identity Theft
Someone is using another person's identity to list properties or pose as an agent.

**Example**: Scammer using real agent's name and license number.

### Spam
Repeated posting of same or similar properties, or irrelevant content.

**Example**: Agent posts 50 identical properties with different locations.

## What Happens After You Report

1. **Acknowledgment**: You receive immediate confirmation
2. **Review**: Our team reviews your report within 24-48 hours
3. **Investigation**: We verify the information and may contact you for clarification
4. **Action**: Depending on findings:
   - Property/Agent may be suspended pending investigation
   - Fraudulent content is removed
   - User accounts may be banned for serious violations
5. **Resolution**: Report is marked as resolved or dismissed

**Note**: For privacy reasons, we may not share all investigation details.

## Privacy & Anonymity

### What Information We Collect

- Your email address (or user account if logged in)
- IP address (for security purposes)
- Report details you provide

### How We Use Your Information

- **Only** for investigating the reported issue
- May contact you for clarification or updates
- Internal records for fraud pattern detection

### What We Don't Do

- ❌ Share your identity with the reported party
- ❌ Sell or share your email with third parties
- ❌ Display your name publicly

### Logged In vs. Anonymous Reporting

**Logged In Users**:
- Faster processing (we can verify your history)
- You can track your reports in your dashboard
- Email is auto-filled from your account

**Anonymous Users**:
- Must provide valid email
- Limited to 3 reports per day (to prevent abuse)
- Cannot track report status

## FAQs

### Q: Will the agent/property owner know I reported them?

**A**: No. Your identity is kept confidential. We never disclose who filed a report.

---

### Q: How long does it take to investigate?

**A**: Most reports are reviewed within 24-48 hours. Complex cases may take 3-5 business days.

---

### Q: Can I report the same issue multiple times?

**A**: Please don't. Duplicate reports slow down our review process. If your initial report hasn't been addressed, contact support@malisafi.com.

---

### Q: What if I made a mistake in my report?

**A**: Email support@malisafi.com with your report details to update or retract it.

---

### Q: Do I get updates on my report?

**A**: Currently, reports are reviewed internally. For status updates, email support@malisafi.com referencing your report ID (if provided in confirmation).

---

### Q: What happens to false reports?

**A**: Intentional false reporting is a violation of our Terms of Service and may result in:
- IP address blocking
- Account suspension
- Legal action in severe cases

---

### Q: I'm being blackmailed with a fake report threat. What should I do?

**A**: Immediately contact support@malisafi.com. Blackmail is illegal and we cooperate with law enforcement.

---

### Q: Can I report someone for poor service?

**A**: No. This system is for **fraud** only. For service complaints, use the agent rating system or contact support.

---

### Q: Is there a reward for reporting fraud?

**A**: We don't offer financial rewards, but you're helping make our platform safer for everyone. Thank you!
